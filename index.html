About WEST

Western Intelligence (WEST) is an independent private intelligence consultancy based in Apple Valley, California. Its work spans maritime tracking telemetry, automated open-source intelligence (OSINT) pipelines, predictive modeling, and asymmetric threat assessment — delivered in plain English, bottom-line-up-front, with verified facts separated from assessment.

WEST's desks include the Analytical Engine, Public Dissemination, Maritime & Subsea Telemetry, Geopolitical Convergence & CENTCOM, and Systems Architecture & Engineering. Systems Architecture is the proof of the work: code on GitHub.

west-predictive-methods

Predictive methods from nonlinear dynamics: phase-space reconstruction, Takens delay embedding, false nearest neighbours, Lyapunov / Rosenstein methods, recurrence quantification analysis, and a phase-space pipeline with Theiler-corrected FNN, PCA, sliding-window twRQA and a Welford bifurcation detector.

Tested on synthetic Lorenz-63 and Rössler benches only — not real telemetry. The ZeroMQ streaming worker has not been runtime-tested. The source export is partial (it ends mid-table); nothing has been invented to fill the gap.
